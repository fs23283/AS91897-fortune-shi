StratMon - Competitive Rules

This page addresses the rules of Smogon. But feel free anytime to investigate Pokémon Showdown yourself! For clarity, the formats are split into 4 main categories, which all belong to Smogon University.

Smogon Tiers

This is the most structured category of Smogon formats. Pokémon are ranked by statistics based on usage competitively, then placed into their individual tiers. The tiers include: OU (OverUsed), UU (UnderUsed), RU (Rarely Used), NU (Never Used), PU, and Ubers.


However, certain Pokémon are banned if they are considered to be too strong for their environment. Examples of this could be:


  • Flutter Mane in OU due to its extreme speed and special attack.
  • Volcarona for its typing and Quiver Dance move.

Random Battles

Random Battles removes the team-building aspect; instead it gives each player a randomly generated team. These teams are roughly balanced, with standard movesets for each Pokémon (which can vary depending on luck). Levels are also adjusted so all Pokémon have even strength.


The aim of this mode is to develop a player's adaptability and in-game descision-making. However, there is still an element of luck involved, as some matchups are favoured more than others.

VGC

VGC (Video Game Championships) is the official competitive format used by the Pokémon Company. It is usually 2v2, where both players bring 4 teams of 6 into a match. There are often limitations and restrictions on certain Pokémon, but this changes each season.


This mode is centered around synergy between Pokémon. Moves like Protect, or speed control moves (Tailwind and Trick Room) are extremely important. VGC is much different than Smogon's singles formats, and can lead to much more intense battles.


Chaos Modes

This mode is centered around unbalanced and unpredictable gameplay. Formats like Anything goes (AG) remove bans, and Hackmon, where Pokémon can have any moves and abilities. These moves often ignore standard rules and can produce very interesting scenarios.


Players usually test absurd Pokémon combinations and movesets, while others opt for full firepower, with strong choices such as Xerneas and Melmetal (who are banned in most gamemodes).


Other Rules - Mods

These are the core "clauses/mods" that are put in place in certain gamemodes, to make it fair for everyone.


  • 😴 Sleep Clause Mod - You can only put one of your opponents Pokémon to sleep at a time.
  • 👥 Species Clause - You cannot have a duplicate Pokémon in a team.
  • 🎯 Evasion Clause - Moves that increase evasion (Double Team, Miminise) are banned.
  • 🎲 Moody Clause - Ability 'Moody' is banned due to random stats boosts being too strong.
  • ♾️ Endless Battle Mod - Prevents infinite battles.

However, there are still other Clauses, such as Dynamax Clause, or Monotype Clause. But these are mostly gamemode-specific.
