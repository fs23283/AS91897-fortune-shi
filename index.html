StratMon - Introduction

Welcome to StratMon! This a fanmade website for the popular battle format played on browser - Pokemon Showdown. The intention of this website is to provide simple information on Smogon battle simulation to any Pokemon fan, or complete newbies.

Note: We do not own any assets that are used within this website, they are all under legal control of Game Freak, All rights reserved ©. For more information navigate to Legal

Brief history outline

The website 'Pokemon Showdown' was originally developed by a small creator Guagcong "Zarel" Luo in 2011. He was inspired by his experience on an older simulator called Pokemon Online. The purpose of 'Pokemon Showdown' was to create a bug free simulator, emcompassing all mechanics for all Pokemon generations, in which users can simulate battles.
