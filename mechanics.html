StratMon - Mechanics

This page will go over basic battling mechanics on - Pokémon Showdown. The aim is for you to instantly jump into a battle, and know how to win.

Hazards

Hazards are setup objects you can place on your opponent's field by using specific moves, this includes:


  • 🪨 Stealth Rock/Spikes - Both inflicts damage when foe switches in a Pokémon.
  • 🕸️ Sticky Web - Lowers opponent's Pokémon by 1 stage upon switch in.
  • 🫟 Toxic Spikes - Poisons/Toxics opponents switch in (depending on 1 or 2 layer).

This is extremely beneficial, as it creates minor disadvantages for your opponent. Hazards can only be removed if you opponent uses moves like Defog, or Rapid Spin.


For a more detailed breakdown, visit the offical Smogon University website.

Stats Boost

A Pokémon can increase/decrease their own stats (reset upon switch-out or switch-in), with moves like:


  • ⚔️ Swords Dance/Nasty Plot - Raises attack/special attack by 2 stages respectively
  • 🪭 Quiver Dance/Dragon Dance - Increases speed and attack/special attack by 1 stage respectively (Quiver Dance also increases special defense by one stage).
  • 🛡️ Iron Defense/Acid Amor - Raises defense/special defense by 2 stages respectively.

This is the most crucial part of Pokémon battle simulation, as it sets up a team to sweep the foe.


However, there are moves like Shell Smash, or Close Combat that lower's the user's stats. For a more detailed breakdown, visit the offical Smogon University website.

Status Effects

Status effects can be inflicted on your foe's Pokémon permanently (or until it runs out), unless they have a way to remove it.


  • 💤 Sleep - Pokémon falls asleep and is unable to move for 1-3 turns.
  • 🦥 Paralysis - Foe's speed is halved, and has a 30% chance to not move each turn.
  • 🔥 Burn - Deals damage each turn, halves, attack physical attack stat.
  • ☢️ Toxic - Toxic does more damage the longer it is inflicted for.
  • 🧊 Freeze - Frozen Pokémon is unable to attack until it is thawed.

This can render a Pokémon useless in battle, reducing options for your opponent. For a more detailed breakdown, visit the official Smogon University website.
